Privacy

HeyKunnamkulam has no accounts, no sign-up and no ads, and we run no analytics.

  • On your device: your trip history is saved in your browser’s local storage so the game can keep a record. It never leaves your device. Clear it any time in your browser settings.
  • Hosting: the site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs such as IP address and browser type.
  • Third parties: the page loads fonts from Google Fonts and the 3D library from cdnjs. Those providers can see your IP address when they serve the files.

If we add accounts or analytics later, this page will say so first. Questions: open an issue on the project’s GitHub repository.
