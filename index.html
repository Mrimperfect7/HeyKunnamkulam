How a trip works

1

Accept a trip

A request arrives with a short countdown. Take it, or skip and wait for the next one.

2

Pick up at the yellow beam

Drive over and ease to a stop. Your passenger climbs in.

3

Drop at the green beam

The fare ticks up as you go. Stop at their stop and collect it.

What is inside today

Busy-hour fares

Some requests pay more. Take the ones worth the drive.

Stars and tips

Calm driving earns a tip. Bumps and a heavy foot lose it.

Take-home against a target

Cover the vehicle cost first, then chase the day’s target.

A town with weather in the light

Shadows, haze and a sun that moves through your shift.

An early build, honestly

This is the first playable version. Traffic lights, rain, radio and saved shifts are not in yet. The town is invented, inspired by Kunnamkulam rather than a map of it.

Price

Free to play. No account, no ads.

Questions

Does it work on my phone?

Yes, in landscape. Hold the phone sideways and use the on-screen pedals and steering arrows. On a computer, use W, A, S, D or the arrow keys.

Is this a real map of Kunnamkulam?

No. The roads and buildings are invented. The setting, light and feel are inspired by Kerala.

Is it free?

Yes, and there is nothing to buy.

Is it connected to a ride-hailing company?

No. It is an independent game and is not affiliated with any ride-hailing company.
